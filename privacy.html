Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how Rally Clipping ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal tool that runs on its operator's own computer. It edits short rally video clips and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner uses the app. It is not offered to the public, and no outside users can create accounts.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish clips the owner has reviewed and approved.
  • View YouTube account details (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is connected and to check upload status.

The app also searches public YouTube videos through the YouTube Data API to find rally footage. No personal data is involved in that search.

How the information is used and stored

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the operator's own computer, and it is used only to upload the owner's approved videos to the owner's channel.
  • No information is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored token stop working, and deleting the app's local files removes it completely.
