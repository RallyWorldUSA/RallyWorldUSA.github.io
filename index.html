Rally Clipping

Rally racing's wildest moments, cut short and built to loop.

Rally Clipping is a personal content tool used by the operator of a rally-highlights channel. It helps edit short vertical rally clips and upload them to the operator's own YouTube channel.

The app is not offered to the public. It runs on the operator's own computer, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

Google / YouTube access

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to that owner's channel and to read basic channel details. It does not access anyone else's account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
